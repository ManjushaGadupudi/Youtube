Format: 60s vertical Shorts, hook → setup → 2 escalating facts → twist → CTA (per `scripts/two-countries-one-town.md` template). One topic per day. Avoids repeats of already-produced videos (Baarle-Nassau/Baarle-Hertog — Day pattern "split town"; Diomede Islands — "date line"/"two countries") and of GeoGlobeTales' own known catalog (Tristan da Cunha, Pheasant Island, Monowi, the Whisky War, the Wallace Line, Märket Island, Slowjamastan, the Bermuda Triangle, the Darien Gap, UK/GB/England, snowiest country).
