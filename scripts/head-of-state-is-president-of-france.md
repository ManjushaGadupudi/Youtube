# Script: "This Country's Head of State Is the President of France"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 13 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Andorra, a co-principality ruled jointly by the Bishop of Urgell and the President of France
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This tiny country has two heads of state at the same time — and one of them isn't even from here."

*VISUAL: A small gold-outlined country nestled between two larger green landmasses in the mountains, two crown-like icons fading in above it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "2 heads of state. 1 country."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "This is Andorra, tucked into the Pyrenees mountains between France and Spain — home to fewer than 80,000 people."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Andorra in gold, France to the north and Spain to the south, mountain-shaded terrain around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Andorra. Between France and Spain."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "It's a co-principality, ruled jointly by two 'princes': the Bishop of Urgell, in Spain, and the President of France, whoever that happens to be."

*VISUAL: Two small icons — a bishop's mitre and a French flag — appear on opposite sides of the Andorra marker, connected by thin gold lines.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Ruled by a bishop and a president."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "That means every time France elects a new president, Andorra automatically gets a new co-prince — no vote, no ceremony required there."

*VISUAL: A small "election" ballot icon flickers near the French flag icon, then a crown icon updates instantly above Andorra.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "New French president. New co-prince."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "This arrangement dates back to 1278 — a deal that's never been fully replaced, just quietly kept running for more than 700 years."

*VISUAL: A timeline arrow stretches back across the screen to "1278," then snaps forward to the present day marker.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "1278. Still running today."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One tiny country, two rulers, one of them a foreign head of state by default — and it's worked for over 700 years. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the full Pyrenees region, gold Andorra nestled between green France and green Spain, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
