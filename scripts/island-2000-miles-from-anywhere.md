# Script: "This Island Is Almost 2,000 Miles From Anywhere"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 15 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Easter Island (Rapa Nui), Chilean territory, one of the most remote inhabited places on Earth
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each, targeting a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting reads correctly.

1. "This Island Is Almost 2,000 Miles From Anywhere 🗿" (primary)
2. "The Loneliest Inhabited Island on Earth"

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This island's nearest inhabited neighbor is over 1,200 miles away. There's more open ocean around it than around almost anywhere else people live."
*VISUAL: Vast blue Pacific Ocean, a single tiny gold-marked dot far from any coastline, camera pulling back to emphasize empty water.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "1,200 miles from anyone."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's Easter Island — Rapa Nui — Chilean territory sitting almost 2,300 miles from mainland Chile, across open Pacific Ocean."
*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Rapa Nui in gold, a dashed line stretching east across blue ocean to a green sliver marked Chile.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "2,300 miles from mainland Chile."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "Its closest neighbor, Pitcairn Island, is still over 1,200 miles west — making this one of the most isolated inhabited places on Earth."
*VISUAL: Dashed line drawing westward from the island marker toward a smaller Pitcairn marker, blue ocean stretching between them.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Closest neighbor: Pitcairn Island."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Despite the isolation, the island holds nearly 1,000 giant stone moai statues, some standing over 30 feet tall, carved centuries ago."
*VISUAL: Statue-emoji marker glowing at the island, small count ticking upward beside it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Nearly 1,000 stone statues."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "They were carved and moved without wheels, metal tools, or draft animals — and archaeologists still argue about exactly how it was done."
*VISUAL: Close zoom on the island marker, a question-mark icon fading in and out beside the statue emoji.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Moved without wheels. Still debated."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "Almost 2,000 miles of open ocean in every direction, nearly 1,000 statues, and a mystery nobody's fully solved. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to the full South Pacific — tiny gold island marker surrounded by vast blue water, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
