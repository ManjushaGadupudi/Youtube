# Script: "Spain Has Two Cities in Africa"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 11 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Ceuta and Melilla, Spanish cities on the North African coast
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. "Spain Has Two Cities in Africa 🇪🇸" (primary)
2. "This Is Spain — But It's Not in Europe"
3. "The EU's Only Land Border With Africa Isn't What You'd Guess"

---

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This is Spain. But it's not in Europe — it's sitting right on the North African coast, bordering Morocco directly."

*VISUAL: Push-in on the African coastline, a small gold-outlined city patch glowing against Morocco's green landmass.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Spain. On the African coast."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "They're called Ceuta and Melilla — two Spanish cities on Moroccan territory, both fully part of Spain, both bordering Morocco on land."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling both cities in gold along the coast, Morocco's green landmass surrounding each on the landward side.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Ceuta. Melilla. Both Spanish."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "That makes them the European Union's only land borders with the African continent — a European border you can walk to from Morocco's own streets."

*VISUAL: A thin EU-gold border line traces both city outlines, small flag icons appearing over each.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The EU's only African border."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Both cities are ringed by tall, layered border fences — among the most heavily fortified land borders anywhere in Europe or Africa."

*VISUAL: A double-line fence icon animates along the border outline of each city, small watch-post markers blinking along it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Ringed by tall fences."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "Morocco has never fully recognized Spain's claim to either city — a territorial dispute that's quietly run for more than 500 years."

*VISUAL: Both city outlines flicker between two colors briefly, a faint timeline arrow stretching back across the screen.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "500 years. Still disputed."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "Two Spanish cities, one African coastline, and a five-century argument hiding in plain sight on the map. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the full Strait of Gibraltar region, Spain's mainland to the north, both city markers glowing gold on the African coast, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
