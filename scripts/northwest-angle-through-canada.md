# Script: "The US State You Can Only Drive To From Canada"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 24 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** The Northwest Angle, Minnesota — the only part of the continental US north of the 49th parallel
**Target length:** ~60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-25 words each (beat 6 runs longer for the CTA), targeting a natural ~8-10s read per beat. Narrator is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine, no need to spell out numbers.

1. "The US State You Can Only Drive To From Canada 🇺🇸🇨🇦" (primary)
2. "This Piece of Minnesota Is Stranded Above the Border"

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This is the only part of the US mainland north of the 49th parallel — and you can't drive there without leaving the country."

*VISUAL: Push-in on a small green pocket of land poking above a straight border line into Canadian territory.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Only reachable through Canada."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's called the Northwest Angle — a remote pocket of Minnesota stranded above the border by an inaccurate 1783 treaty map."

*VISUAL: Map zoom on Lake of the Woods, gold outline on the small US pocket, Manitoba and Ontario labeled pressing in around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Stranded by an 1783 map."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "Treaty negotiators assumed the Mississippi's headwaters sat near here. They didn't — and by the time surveyors found the mistake, the border was already law."

*VISUAL: Animated dashed line showing the treaty's intended path, fading into the real jagged border that loops the Angle north of the 49th parallel.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A treaty mistake."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Today, the only land route in cuts straight through Manitoba, Canada — drive south again, and you're back in the United States."

*VISUAL: Route line animating from the Minnesota mainland, dipping north through Manitoba, then back down into the stranded pocket.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Drive through Canada to get home."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "There's no border agent. Locals use an honor-system phone booth to call in and self-report when they cross back into the US."

*VISUAL: Close zoom on a small booth marker at the border crossing, a phone icon glowing, checkmark appearing as if self-reported.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "An honor-system phone booth."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One inaccurate line on a 1783 map, and Americans still cross an international border twice just to reach their own state. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the full US-Canada border, gold pocket glowing above the line, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
