## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "There's a place on Earth where your passport doesn't matter. Any citizen of any country can move here, work here, and live here — no visa required."
*VISUAL: Wide Arctic map, camera pushing in toward a small archipelago near the top of the globe.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "No visa required. Anyone."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's Svalbard — a Norwegian archipelago near the North Pole, open to any nationality since a treaty signed back in 1920."
*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Svalbard in gold, a dashed line reaching up from mainland Norway toward the archipelago.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Open to anyone since 1920."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "The catch: there's no welfare system for non-Norwegians here, so you need a job and a place to live already lined up before you land."
*VISUAL: Marker over Longyearbyen with a briefcase icon fading in beside it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "No welfare. Bring a job."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Around 2,500 people live here — and polar bears in the surrounding region actually outnumber them, so locals carry rifles outside town."
*VISUAL: Marker glowing with a polar bear icon beside the settlement marker.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Polar bears outnumber people."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "Buried in a mountain nearby is the Global Seed Vault — backup seeds from nearly every country on Earth, built to survive a global catastrophe."
*VISUAL: Marker at a mountain vault entrance, seed/sprout icon glowing, tunnel-like shape suggested into the rock.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Backup seeds for the whole planet."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "No visa, polar bears outnumbering people, and the planet's backup seed supply buried underground — this Arctic archipelago has it all. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to full Arctic map — gold Svalbard marker near the pole, green Norway stretching south below it, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
