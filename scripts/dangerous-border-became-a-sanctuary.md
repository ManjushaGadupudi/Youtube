# Script: "The Most Dangerous Border on Earth Became a Wildlife Sanctuary"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 10 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** The Korean Demilitarized Zone (DMZ)
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

---

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. "The Most Dangerous Border on Earth Became a Wildlife Sanctuary 🦌" (primary)
2. "Millions of Landmines. Also One of Asia's Richest Habitats."
3. "No Human Has Lived Here Since 1953 — So Wildlife Moved In"

---

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This is the most heavily fortified border in the world — millions of landmines, guard posts every few hundred meters, soldiers on permanent alert."

*VISUAL: A thin red-gold band glowing across the Korean peninsula, small warning-style icons flickering along its length.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The most fortified border on Earth."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's the Korean DMZ — a 160-mile-long strip separating North and South Korea, created by the 1953 armistice that paused, but never ended, the war."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling North Korea and South Korea, a dashed line running coast to coast between them, a "1953" date stamp fading in.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "160 miles. Since 1953."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "It's only about 2.5 miles wide — but it's arguably the single most dangerous strip of land on the planet, sealed off from almost all human contact."

*VISUAL: The band narrows into a tight highlighted strip, small landmine-style icons dotting it faintly, then fading.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "2.5 miles. Sealed off completely."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "And because almost no human has set foot inside since 1953, it's quietly become one of the richest wildlife habitats left in Asia."

*VISUAL: Small animal icons — a crane, a deer — fading in gently along the strip as the harsh red tone softens toward green.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "No people. So wildlife moved in."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "Endangered red-crowned cranes winter here every year, and some researchers believe a species as rare as the Amur leopard may still survive inside."

*VISUAL: A crane icon and a faint leopard silhouette both glow briefly along the strip, camera holding steady on the band.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Cranes. Maybe even leopards."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "The most dangerous 2.5 miles on the planet turned out to be one of its most untouched. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the full Korean peninsula, the DMZ strip glowing gold between two green landmasses, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
