# Script: "The Airport Runway You Have to Stop Traffic to Use"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 12 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Gibraltar International Airport's runway crossing Winston Churchill Avenue
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This runway crosses the only road in and out of the entire territory. When a plane needs it, the road simply closes."

*VISUAL: A gold runway strip glows across a thin peninsula, a road line running straight through the middle of it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "One road. One runway. Same spot."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "This is Gibraltar's airport, at the northern tip of the territory — its runway intersects Winston Churchill Avenue, the main road connecting Gibraltar to Spain."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Gibraltar and the airport, the road line tracing north toward the Spanish border.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The road to Spain crosses it."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "Every time a plane takes off or lands, movable barriers swing shut across the road — and cars simply stop, right in the middle of it."

*VISUAL: Small barrier-gate icons animate closed across the road line, a row of tiny car icons pausing in place.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Barriers close. Cars just wait."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "It happens several times a day, every day — pedestrians and drivers treating an active international airport runway like a regular traffic light."

*VISUAL: A small clock icon ticks forward rapidly, the barrier animation repeating a few times in quick succession.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Several times. Every single day."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "In 2023, Gibraltar finally opened a tunnel running underneath the runway — but the road-crossing barriers are still there, and still used constantly."

*VISUAL: A thin dashed tunnel line appears beneath the runway strip, while the surface barrier icons keep blinking on top.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A tunnel now exists. Barriers too."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One runway, one road, and a barrier system that turns landing a plane into a full stop for traffic. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the full Gibraltar peninsula, runway and road crossing clearly visible, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*

## VISUAL STYLE DIRECTION (continue established look)
- Same **blue-water / green-land** classic map palette locked across the series. Gibraltar is British territory and too small to be its own polygon in the map dataset at this resolution (it renders as part of the Iberian coastline), so it's marked with a gold marker, a hand-drawn peninsula outline, and a `lines` entry for the runway/road crossing — the same "not a country" convention used for Sealand and Ceuta/Melilla, layered near Spain's real coordinates.
