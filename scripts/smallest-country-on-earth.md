# Script: "The Smallest Country on Earth Is Smaller Than Most Parks"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 5 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Vatican City, the smallest sovereign state on Earth, inside Rome
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting ("0.17 square miles") is fine, no need to spell numbers out.

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. "The Smallest Country on Earth Is Smaller Than Most Parks 🇻🇦" (primary)
2. "This Entire Country Is 0.17 Square Miles"
3. "You Can Walk Across This Country in 15 Minutes"

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This entire country is 0.17 square miles. You could walk across the whole thing, border to border, in about fifteen minutes."

*VISUAL: Tiny gold-outlined patch glowing inside the much larger green sprawl of Rome, slow push-in from a wide city view.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "0.17 square miles. Whole country."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "This is Vatican City — the smallest sovereign state on Earth, sitting entirely inside the city of Rome, surrounded on every side by Italy."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Vatican City in gold, Rome's street grid fading in around it, Italy's green landmass extending to the coast.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Smaller than most city parks."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "Fewer than 1,000 people live there — and yet it has its own train station, its own postal service, and its own postage stamps."

*VISUAL: Small icons animating in near the marker — a train, a stamp, a mailbox — each popping up inside the tiny gold boundary.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Its own trains. Its own stamps."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "It even has its own army: the Swiss Guard, founded in 1506, the oldest continuously operating military unit anywhere in the world."

*VISUAL: A small halberd/shield icon glowing at the Vatican marker, a timeline tick animating back to "1506."*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The world's oldest standing army."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "Despite the size, it's a fully sovereign nation, recognized around the world — a real country, with real borders, smaller than most parking lots."

*VISUAL: Thin gold border pulses once around the tiny shape, a small flag icon rising at its center, camera holding tight on the boundary.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Fully sovereign. Fully real."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One square-ish block, one entire country, one army older than most nations themselves. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — tiny gold Vatican City inside green Rome inside Italy's coastline, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
