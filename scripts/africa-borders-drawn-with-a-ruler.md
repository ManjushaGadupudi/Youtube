# Script: "Africa's Borders Were Drawn With a Ruler"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 21 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Africa's straight-line colonial borders, decided at the 1884 Berlin Conference
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each, targeting a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting reads correctly.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "Look closely at a map of Africa and you'll see it — dozens of perfectly straight lines slicing across the entire continent."
*VISUAL: Camera pans across a wide map of Africa, several gold-outlined countries with visibly straight edges glowing in sequence.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Dozens of perfectly straight borders."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "Most were decided at the 1884 Berlin Conference, by European powers who had barely set foot on the land they were dividing."
*VISUAL: Marker over Berlin fading in, a dashed line sweeping down across Africa as if drawn from that point.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Decided in Berlin. In 1884."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "Those straight lines cut through rivers, mountain ranges, and ethnic groups that had nothing to do with drawing them."
*VISUAL: Gold straight-line borders highlighted across Egypt, Libya, Chad, and Niger, cutting visibly across natural terrain.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Cutting through rivers and peoples."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Nearly half of Africa's modern borders are still perfectly straight lines today, more than anywhere else on Earth."
*VISUAL: Multiple gold-outlined countries pulsing in sequence across the continent, straight edges emphasized.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Nearly half are still straight lines."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "One border between Namibia and Botswana runs so precisely straight that it was fixed with a single line of longitude on a map."
*VISUAL: Close zoom on the Namibia-Botswana border, a straight gold line running cleanly along a longitude gridline.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "One line of longitude. A border."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "A continent's borders, ruled straight by people who'd never walked the land — the ruler marks of a meeting none of them attended. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to the full continent, several straight-edged countries glowing gold at once, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
