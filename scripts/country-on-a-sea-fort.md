# Script: "Someone Built a Country on an Abandoned Sea Fort"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 6 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** The Principality of Sealand, a micronation on a WWII anti-aircraft platform in the North Sea
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting ("1967", "7 miles") is fine.

2. "This 'Country' Is a WWII Gun Platform in the North Sea"

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "In 1967, a man sailed out to an abandoned WWII anti-aircraft platform in the North Sea, climbed aboard, and declared it a country."

*VISUAL: Small boat icon crossing blue open water toward a lone gold-marked platform dot, zooming in slowly.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "He declared it a country."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's called the Principality of Sealand — a concrete platform the size of two tennis courts, 7 miles off the English coast."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling the platform in gold, England's coastline visible in the distance, blue North Sea surrounding it on all sides.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The size of two tennis courts."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "And it plays the part completely: Sealand has its own flag, its own currency, and its own passports, all issued from that one platform."

*VISUAL: Small icons animating in above the platform marker — a flag, a coin, a passport booklet.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Its own flag. Currency. Passports."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "It even has a royal family. The Bates family has ruled Sealand for generations, surviving coup attempts, a fire, and decades on their own."

*VISUAL: A small crown icon glowing above the platform marker, a faint flicker/fire texture animating briefly then fading.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A royal family. On a platform."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "No country on Earth formally recognizes Sealand as a nation. But in nearly 60 years, no country has managed to shut it down either."

*VISUAL: Gold outline pulses once around the tiny platform shape, camera holding steady, blue water stretching to the horizon on every side.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Unrecognized. Unremovable."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One abandoned fort, one declared nation, nearly 60 years of surviving on a concrete platform in the middle of the sea. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — tiny gold platform marker in the blue North Sea, England's coastline in the distance, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*

## VISUAL STYLE DIRECTION (continue established look)
