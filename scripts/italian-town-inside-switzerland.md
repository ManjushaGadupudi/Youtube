## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This town is officially part of Italy. But to drive anywhere, you have to leave Italy first — because Switzerland surrounds it completely."

*VISUAL: Small green landmass patch entirely enclosed by a larger green landmass, blue lake visible at one edge.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Italian soil. Surrounded by Switzerland."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's called Campione d'Italia, sitting on Lake Lugano — a tiny Italian exclave completely cut off from the rest of Italy by Swiss territory."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Campione in gold, blue lake wrapping one edge, green Swiss landmass surrounding the rest.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Campione d'Italia. Cut off from Italy."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "The nearest Italian border crossing is over an hour away by mountain roads — so locals just use Swiss license plates, Swiss phone codes, and Swiss francs."

*VISUAL: Route line from the town winding through green mountainous terrain toward a distant Italian border marker, far longer than a direct line to Switzerland.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Swiss francs. Swiss plates. Italian passports."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "For decades this quirk let the town run one of Europe's biggest casinos completely outside Italian gambling law — because Swiss rules applied instead."

*VISUAL: Casino-chip icon glowing at the town marker, a small border-line icon fading between the two countries around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Italian law didn't reach here."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "In 2020, Italy finally folded the town into the EU customs zone with Switzerland — ending a legal gray area that had lasted over 150 years."

*VISUAL: Timeline animation, a border-line icon flickering between two states before settling, dates ticking forward.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "150 years of legal gray area."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "Italian on paper, Swiss in practice, surrounded on every side — for a century and a half, this tiny town played by its own rules. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — green Campione fully enclosed in green Switzerland, blue lake at its edge, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
