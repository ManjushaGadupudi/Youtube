# Script: "This Country Is Completely Inside Another Country"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 2 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Lesotho, entirely surrounded by South Africa
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Each line below is pre-tuned to ~19-21 words, targeting a natural ~8-9s read to fit the assembler's ~7.8-9.5s/block window on the first take and cut down regeneration retries.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This country has exactly one neighbor. Not because it's small — because that one neighbor completely surrounds it, on every single side."

*VISUAL: Green landmass shape fully enclosed by a larger green landmass, blue coastline visible far around the outer edge.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "One country. Zero other neighbors."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "This is Lesotho. A mountain kingdom sitting entirely inside the borders of South Africa — the only fully enclosed country in Africa."

*VISUAL: Map zoom highlighting Lesotho's border outline in gold, South Africa's green landmass wrapping completely around it, ocean in blue at the continent's edges.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Lesotho. Fully enclosed."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "It's also one of only three countries on Earth completely surrounded by a single other nation — alongside San Marino and Vatican City."

*VISUAL: Three small highlighted dots appear on a world map — Lesotho, San Marino, Vatican City — each glowing gold inside a larger green host country.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Only 3 countries like this exist."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Lesotho sits so high in the mountains that it's the only country on Earth entirely above 1,000 meters in elevation."

*VISUAL: Terrain-style elevation map, green landmass rising into shaded mountain peaks, an elevation line animating upward across the whole country.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Entirely above 1,000 meters."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "That height means Lesotho exports something South Africa doesn't have enough of — water, piped downhill straight into Johannesburg's taps."

*VISUAL: Blue water-pipeline icon animating from Lesotho's mountains down across the border into a South African city marker.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "It exports water. Downhill."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One country, one neighbor, completely surrounded — and still exporting the one thing its giant neighbor needs most. Follow for more countries that shouldn't work, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — green Lesotho fully enclosed in green South Africa, blue coastline ringing the continent's edge, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
