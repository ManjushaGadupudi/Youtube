# Script: "This Island Is Split Between Two Countries — Again"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 8 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Usedom, a Baltic Sea island divided between Germany and Poland
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. "This Island Is Split Between Two Countries — Again 🇩🇪🇵🇱" (primary)
2. "One Beach, Two Countries, No Checkpoint"
3. "This Island Changed Hands Without Anyone Moving"

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "One island. One beach. Two countries. You can walk across the border here without ever seeing a checkpoint."

*VISUAL: Aerial push-in over a sandy Baltic coastline, a faint dotted line running straight across the beach, no gate or fence in sight.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "No checkpoint. Just a line."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "This is Usedom, on the Baltic Sea — divided between Germany and the Polish city of Świnoujście, right at the island's eastern edge."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling the island, Germany's green landmass on the west side, Poland's on the east, a thin border line running through the middle.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Split: Germany west, Poland east."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "About 80% of the island is German. The rest, including its biggest town, belongs to Poland — split by a border that only shows up on maps."

*VISUAL: Shading animates across the island, most of it in one shade, a smaller eastern wedge in another, a city marker glowing at the edge.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "80% Germany. 20% Poland."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "It's one of dozens of quiet, unmarked land borders across Europe's Schengen Zone — places where you can cross a country line without slowing down."

*VISUAL: Small dotted lines fade in briefly across a wider map of Europe, then fade back to focus on Usedom's own line.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "One of dozens like it."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "Walk from the German side to the Polish side on the sand, and the only thing that changes is the flag on the beach umbrellas."

*VISUAL: A single footprint icon crossing the dotted beach line, two small flag icons flipping from one side to the other.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The border is the umbrellas."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One island, one beach, two countries, and a border you could miss completely. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — green Germany and green Poland meeting on the island, blue Baltic Sea around it, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
