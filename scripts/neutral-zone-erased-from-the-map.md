# Script: "The Country-Sized Zone That No Longer Exists on Any Map"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 27 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** The old Saudi Arabia–Iraq Neutral Zone (1922–1991)
**Target length:** ~60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~20-25 words each (beat 6 runs longer for the CTA), targeting a natural ~8-10s read per beat. Narrator is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine, no need to spell out numbers.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "For most of the 20th century, this desert belonged to no country. It was a neutral zone between Iraq and Saudi Arabia."

*VISUAL: Push-in over open desert terrain between two labeled countries, a faint gold outline tracing a kite-shaped zone between them.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A desert with no country."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "In 1922, British and Saudi negotiators created it so nomadic tribes could cross the desert freely, no fences, no checkpoints, no fixed border."

*VISUAL: Map zoom on the Arabian Peninsula's northern edge, the kite-shaped zone drawing itself in gold between Iraq and Saudi Arabia.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Created for nomads in 1922."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "The Neutral Zone covered about 7,000 square kilometers — roughly the size of a small country, belonging to neither Iraq nor Saudi Arabia."

*VISUAL: Tight zoom on the gold-outlined zone, a small country-shaped comparison icon fading in and out beside it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "7,000 sq km, owned by no one."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "By the 1980s, oil interests and modern border politics made a shared no-man's-land impossible to maintain, so both countries agreed to just split it."

*VISUAL: Small oil-derrick icon appearing inside the zone, a dashed line animating down the middle as if slicing the shape in two.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Too complicated to keep."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "The zone was divided in 1982 and formally dissolved by 1991, absorbed so completely that it doesn't appear on any modern map at all."

*VISUAL: The gold outline fades away entirely as the dashed split line becomes a solid ordinary border, leaving no trace of the original shape.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Erased from the map by 1991."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "A country-sized piece of desert that belonged to no one, quietly erased by two governments and a straight line on a map. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the wider Middle East map, ordinary borders where the zone used to be, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
