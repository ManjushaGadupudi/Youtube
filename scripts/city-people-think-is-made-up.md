# Script: "The City People Think Is Made Up — But Isn't"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 20 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Timbuktu, Mali — real city, former center of Islamic scholarship and the gold/salt trade
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each, targeting a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting reads correctly.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "People use this city's name as a joke for the middle of nowhere. It's real, and it used to be one of the richest cities on Earth."
*VISUAL: Camera pushes into a marker on the edge of the Sahara, a small desert-texture backdrop suggested around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "It's real. And it was rich."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's Timbuktu, in modern-day Mali, sitting near the Niger River on the southern edge of the Sahara Desert."
*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Timbuktu in gold, the Niger River line curling nearby, green Mali surrounding it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Timbuktu, Mali. On the Sahara's edge."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "For centuries it was a major center of the gold and salt trade, funneling caravan wealth across the desert and into the wider world."
*VISUAL: Small caravan-route line animating toward the marker, gold-coin icon glowing briefly at Timbuktu.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A capital of gold and salt trade."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "It was also a major center of Islamic scholarship, home to libraries holding hundreds of thousands of ancient manuscripts."
*VISUAL: Marker with a book-stack icon, small page/document shapes fading in around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Hundreds of thousands of manuscripts."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "In 2012, when militants threatened to destroy them, residents secretly smuggled most of those manuscripts out of the city to save them."
*VISUAL: Small route line animating away from the marker, a document icon moving along it to safety.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Smuggled out to save them."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "A real population, a real airport, and a real history stranger than the legend — Timbuktu was never the middle of nowhere. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to full map of West Africa, gold Timbuktu marker on green Mali, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
