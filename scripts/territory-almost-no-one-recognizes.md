# Script: "The Territory Almost No Country Recognizes"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 17 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Nagorno-Karabakh, a mountainous region within Azerbaijan's internationally recognized borders, disputed for decades
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**Sensitivity note (live geopolitical topic):** This script stays strictly factual and neutral — no editorializing about who is "right," no taking a side on sovereignty. It sticks to the same neutral framing used in `video-titles-descriptions.md`'s Day 17 description: Nagorno-Karabakh lies within Azerbaijan's internationally recognized borders, was long home to a majority-Armenian population and a self-declared unrecognized republic, and after renewed conflict in 2020 and 2023 the self-declared republic dissolved and nearly its entire Armenian population left. Every claim below is stated as an observable fact (recognized borders, population history, dates, outcomes), not an opinion.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each, targeting a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting reads correctly.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "For decades, this mountainous region sat at the center of one of the world's longest-running territorial disputes — and the line kept moving."
*VISUAL: Camera pushes into a mountainous region inside Azerbaijan's outline, a faint gold boundary shape pulsing to mark the disputed area.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A decades-long territorial dispute."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "Nagorno-Karabakh lies within Azerbaijan's internationally recognized borders, but for generations it was home to a majority-Armenian population."
*VISUAL: Map zoom on the gold-outlined region inside green Azerbaijan, Armenia's border visible just to the west.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Inside Azerbaijan. Majority-Armenian population."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "After the Soviet Union collapsed, war broke out in the 1990s, leaving the region controlled by a self-declared republic no UN member recognized."
*VISUAL: Marker at Stepanakert labeled with the region's name, a small flag-outline icon fading in and out to suggest disputed status.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A republic no country recognized."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "A second war in 2020 shifted large parts of the territory back toward Azerbaijani control, ending in a Russian-brokered ceasefire."
*VISUAL: Boundary line redrawing partway across the gold-outlined shape, dates ticking forward on screen.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "2020: the line shifted again."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "In 2023, renewed conflict led nearly the entire Armenian population, around 100,000 people, to leave within days — the republic dissolved in 2024."
*VISUAL: Marker at Stepanakert with a small departing-arrow icon, population count fading toward zero.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "100,000 people left within days."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "A mountain region, three wars, one dissolved republic — a reminder of how fast a line on a map can reshape real lives. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to show Azerbaijan and Armenia together, the now-faded gold outline over the region, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
