# Script: "The Piece of Land No Country Wants"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 7 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Bir Tawil, a stretch of desert between Egypt and Sudan claimed by neither country
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

2. "This Desert Belongs to Absolutely No One"

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This is one of the only pieces of land on Earth that belongs to absolutely no country — and both neighbors could claim it, but won't."

*VISUAL: A dusty tan-gold patch of desert on the map, glowing faintly, no flag icon appearing over it, unlike the countries around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Belongs to no country. On purpose."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's called Bir Tawil, a stretch of desert sitting right on the border between Egypt and Sudan, about 800 square miles of unclaimed land."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling Egypt and Sudan in green on either side, a gold quadrilateral outline drawn between them along the border.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "800 square miles. No owner."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "Nearby sits the real prize: the Hala'ib Triangle, a much larger, far more valuable stretch of land that both Egypt and Sudan actively claim."

*VISUAL: A second, larger shape appears northeast of Bir Tawil, pulsing with both a green and a gold outline overlapping to show dual claims.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Hala'ib Triangle. Claimed by both."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Here's the catch: claiming Bir Tawil under international law would mean legally giving up the claim to Hala'ib. So neither country will touch it."

*VISUAL: Two arrows animate from Egypt and Sudan toward Bir Tawil, then both retract and point instead toward the Hala'ib Triangle shape.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Claim one, lose the other."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "So it just sits there: real land, no government, no owner — occasionally 'claimed' online by random adventurers who plant a flag and leave."

*VISUAL: A tiny flag-planting icon flickers briefly over the gold Bir Tawil shape, then fades away, leaving it blank again.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "'Claimed' online. Not really."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "Two countries, one strip of desert, and a legal loophole that keeps it unclaimed on purpose. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — gold Bir Tawil quadrilateral between green Egypt and green Sudan, blue Red Sea in the distance, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
