# Script: "The World's Oldest Republic Is Smaller Than Most Cities"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 14 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** San Marino, entirely surrounded by Italy, claimed oldest continuously sovereign state on Earth
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each, targeting a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is now Google TTS, so normal digit formatting (301, 1600) reads correctly — no need to spell numbers out.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This country was founded in the year 301. It has outlasted the fall of Rome, the Renaissance, and every empire that came after."
*VISUAL: Tiny gold-outlined patch of land on a mountaintop, fully enclosed by green Italian territory, slow push-in.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Founded in the year 301."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's called San Marino — a mountain republic completely surrounded by Italy, and never conquered in over 1,700 years of existing."
*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling San Marino in gold on Mount Titano, green Italian landmass wrapping it on every side.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Surrounded by Italy. Never conquered."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "San Marino calls itself the oldest continuously sovereign state on Earth — older than the Vatican, older than every modern nation."
*VISUAL: Timeline animation ticking backward past centuries to a founding marker at Mount Titano, gold highlight pulsing.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "The oldest republic on Earth."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Its constitution was written in 1600 — and parts of it are technically still in force today, one of the oldest governing documents in use."
*VISUAL: Old parchment-style document icon fading into the modern map marker, date stamp ticking from 1600 to present.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A 1600 constitution. Still in force."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "With around 33,000 people, San Marino somehow has more registered cars than residents — one of the highest car-to-person ratios on Earth."
*VISUAL: Small car icons multiplying and overflowing past a population-count icon at the country marker.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "More cars than people."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One mountaintop, 1,700 years, and never a single conqueror — the oldest republic on Earth is barely the size of a small town. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — gold San Marino marker on Mount Titano, green Italy surrounding it entirely, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
