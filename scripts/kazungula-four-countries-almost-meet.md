# Script: "The One Spot Where Four Countries Almost Meet"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 28 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** The Kazungula quadripoint (Botswana / Zambia / Zimbabwe / Namibia), Zambezi River
**Target length:** ~60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~20-25 words each (beat 6 runs longer for the CTA), targeting a natural ~8-10s read per beat. Narrator is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine, no need to spell out numbers.

---

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. "The One Spot Where Four Countries Almost Meet 🌍" (primary)
2. "Four Countries, One River, Almost One Point"
3. "The Border Spot International Law Says Can't Exist"

---

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "Four countries almost touch at this single spot on the Zambezi River — the closest thing on Earth to a true four-country meeting point."

*VISUAL: Push-in over the Zambezi River in southern Africa, four gold-outlined countries pressing toward one glowing point.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Four countries. One point."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "Botswana, Zambia, Zimbabwe, and Namibia all converge here, at Kazungula, where the Zambezi and Chobe rivers meet in southern Africa."

*VISUAL: Map zoom labeling all four countries around a shared river confluence, gold marker glowing at the meeting point.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Botswana · Zambia · Zimbabwe · Namibia"*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "International law technically doesn't recognize true quadripoints, so surveyors have argued for decades over exactly which two countries touch in the middle."

*VISUAL: Extreme zoom on the meeting point, four thin gold lines converging, a small magnifying-glass icon hovering over the exact center.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Surveyors still argue over this."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "For decades, a car ferry was the only way to cross between them, travelers could hop borders in minutes, where four nations nearly meet."

*VISUAL: A small ferry icon animating back and forth across the river between two gold-outlined banks.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A ferry between 4 countries."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "In 2021, a new bridge finally replaced the ferry, but the old survey dispute never got resolved, it's really two tripoints, not one true quadripoint."

*VISUAL: A bridge icon animating into place over the river where the ferry used to run, the four converging lines splitting into two separate junction points.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Actually two tripoints, not one."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "Four countries, one river, and a border puzzle surveyors still can't fully agree on. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to the full southern Africa map, all four countries gold-outlined around the river, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
