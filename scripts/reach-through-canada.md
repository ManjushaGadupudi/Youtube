# Script: "You Can Only Reach This Part of the US Through Canada"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 1 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Point Roberts, Washington, USA
**Target length:** ~60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

---

## TITLE OPTIONS
1. "You Can Only Reach This Part of the US Through Canada 🇺🇸🇨🇦" (primary)
2. "This Is America — But You Have to Cross Canada to Get There"
3. "The US Town Cut Off From the Rest of the Country"

---

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "This is the United States. To drive here from the rest of the United States, you have to leave the country first."

*VISUAL: Push-in on a green peninsula dipping into blue water, US flag fading in over it, then a car icon crossing a border line twice.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "You have to leave the US to get here."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's called Point Roberts. A five-square-mile piece of Washington State, sitting on a peninsula that dips just below the 49th parallel — the line the US and Canada agreed on."

*VISUAL: Map zoom showing the 49th-parallel border line drawn straight across, then the peninsula dipping south of it into blue water on three sides.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "It dips 5 miles south of the border."</br>*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "That five miles was enough. When the border was drawn as a straight line in 1846, nobody adjusted for this tiny piece of land poking below it — so it became American, cut off from America by land."

*VISUAL: Animated ruler drawing the straight 49th-parallel line across the map, then highlighting the small green nub left stranded below it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "A straight line stranded it."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Today, about 1,000 people live there. To drive in from Seattle, you cross into Canada, drive through British Columbia, and cross back into the US — twice through customs for one trip."

*VISUAL: Route line animating from a Seattle marker, dipping up through Canadian territory, then back down into the green peninsula, with two small border-checkpoint icons.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Customs. Twice. For one trip."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "Kids who live there ride a school bus that crosses an international border every single morning — just to reach a school in the same country they already live in."

*VISUAL: School bus icon crossing the animated border line back and forth, small flag icons blinking on each pass.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Crosses a border. To get to school."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "One straight line on a map in 1846, and a thousand people still cross an international border just to go home. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to full map — blue water surrounding the green peninsula on three sides, both flags, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
