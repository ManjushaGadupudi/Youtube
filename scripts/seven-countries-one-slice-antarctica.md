# Script: "Seven Countries Claim the Same Slice of Antarctica"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 9 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Overlapping territorial claims in Antarctica (UK, Chile, Argentina)
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline (cost note):** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each for a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting is fine.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "No one owns Antarctica. But seven countries have officially claimed slices of it anyway — and some of those slices overlap completely."

*VISUAL: A white-gold continent glowing at the bottom of the globe, several translucent wedge shapes fading in on top of each other.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "No owner. Seven claims anyway."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "Each claim is a wedge running from the coast to the South Pole, like a slice of pie — one country's slice per claim, in theory."

*VISUAL: Map zoom on the Antarctic Peninsula, pie-slice-shaped dashed lines radiating from a central pole marker down to the coastline.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Slices of pie, pointing at the Pole."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "The UK, Chile, and Argentina all claim the exact same wedge: the Antarctic Peninsula, the part of the continent closest to South America."

*VISUAL: Three overlapping dashed wedge outlines stack on top of each other over the peninsula, each pulsing gold in turn.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "3 countries. 1 wedge."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "Their claims overlap by tens of thousands of square miles — the same ground claimed three separate times, by three separate governments."

*VISUAL: The three wedge outlines flash one after another, then all three hold on screen at once, clearly stacked.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Same ground. 3 different owners."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "The 1959 Antarctic Treaty froze every claim in place — no new ones, no enforcing old ones — making this the largest land on Earth with no real owner."

*VISUAL: A translucent freeze/ice texture washes once over the whole continent, all wedge lines locking in place and dimming slightly.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Frozen. Literally and legally."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "Seven claims, three of them stacked on the same wedge, and a treaty that just leaves it all in limbo. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."

*VISUAL: Pull back to a full view of Antarctica, wedge outlines fading to a soft gold glow, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*

## VISUAL STYLE DIRECTION (continue established look)
- Same **blue-water / green-land** classic map palette locked across the series, with Antarctica itself rendered as land (it IS present as a landmass in the map dataset, unlike Sealand/Bir Tawil).
