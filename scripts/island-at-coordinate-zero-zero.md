# Script: "There's an 'Island' at Coordinate Zero, Zero — And It's Not Real"

**Format reference:** GeoGlobeTales-style short (map-animation + VO, 60s, YouTube Shorts/TikTok) — Day 22 of 30-day lineup
**Topic:** Null Island, the fictional landmass mapping software sometimes plots at 0°N, 0°E
**Target length:** 60 seconds (6 beats x 10s)
**Tone:** Calm, confident, deadpan-surprised narrator. Short declarative sentences.

**VO word-count discipline:** Lines pre-tuned to ~19-23 words each, targeting a natural ~8-9s read. Narrator engine is Google TTS — normal digit formatting reads correctly.

## SCRIPT (6 x 10s beats)

**Beat 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "Mapping software sometimes shows an island exactly at zero degrees latitude, zero degrees longitude, sitting in open ocean. It's not real."
*VISUAL: Camera pushes into a single point marker on plain blue ocean, no land anywhere in the frame.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "An island at zero, zero."*

**Beat 2 — SETUP (0:10–0:20)**
VO: "It's nicknamed Null Island — what happens when broken location data defaults to that coordinate origin point and gets plotted like a real place."
*VISUAL: Marker labeled at the exact origin point, small glitch-style flicker effect suggested around it.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Broken data. Plotted as a place."*

**Beat 3 — ESCALATION 1 (0:20–0:30)**
VO: "It sits in the Gulf of Guinea, off West Africa — the nearest real coastline is still hundreds of miles away in every direction."
*VISUAL: Dashed line drawing from the ocean point north to the West African coastline, distance implied by empty blue water.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Hundreds of miles from any coast."*

**Beat 4 — ESCALATION 2 (0:30–0:40)**
VO: "The only thing actually there is a real scientific buoy, quietly collecting ocean and weather data for researchers."
*VISUAL: Marker with a buoy icon bobbing gently in place at the exact point.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Just a buoy. That's it."*

**Beat 5 — TWIST / CLIMAX (0:40–0:50)**
VO: "So many broken records point here that mapmakers gave it its own joke flag — some databases have even listed it with a population."
*VISUAL: Marker with a small flag icon fading in beside it, a tiny fake population counter ticking briefly.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Its own flag. A fake population."*

**Beat 6 — BUTTON / CTA (0:50–1:00)**
VO: "No land, no people, just a coordinate, a buoy, and a running joke among mapmakers everywhere. Follow for more places on Earth that shouldn't exist, but do."
*VISUAL: Pull back to a wide Atlantic view — Africa's coastline visible far in the distance, a single gold marker alone in blue ocean, freeze on title card.*
*ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Follow for more 🌍"*
